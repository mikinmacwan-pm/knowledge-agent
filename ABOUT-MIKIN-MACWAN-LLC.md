## About this case study

Knowledge Agent demonstrates **how I lead 0→1 AI product work** for high-stakes frontline support environments:

- Full product lifecycle documentation (strategy → PRD → evals → rollout)
- Product artifacts, not slides alone (golden dataset, sample corpus, UI specs, eval runs)
- Explicit non-goals and disclaimers—no overclaiming deployment or impact

**All scenarios, organizations, and metrics are fictional or sample data.** No confidential client, employer, or nonprofit information is used.
