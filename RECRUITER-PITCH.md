## 150-word version

Knowledge Agent is an anonymized AI Product Management portfolio project: a human-in-the-loop retrieval system for high-stakes decision support. Frontline staff in crisis-adjacent environments need approved information fast—while a caller waits. This case study documents how I would build that capability responsibly: retrieval from governed sources only, mandatory citations, confidence scoring, and escalation when the system should not guess.

The portfolio includes strategy, user research plan, PRD, RAG architecture, a 30-document sample corpus, a 50-row golden evaluation dataset, scored eval runs, release gates, metrics dashboard specs, and Figma-ready UI copy. I emphasize cite-or-silence, human decision authority, and eval-before-expansion—not chatbot theater.

All scenarios, resources, and metrics are fictional or clearly labeled sample data. This demonstrates AI PM thinking for trust-first 0→1 products; it does not claim production deployment or measured impact.

## 2-minute interview pitch

"Frontline staff in high-stakes support environments often search four or six systems while someone is on the phone. The content exists—it's the retrieval, trust, and workflow that break down.

Knowledge Agent is my portfolio answer: a human-in-the-loop retrieval system. Staff ask a natural-language question; the system retrieves from an approved corpus, returns a cited summary with a confidence score, and escalates when it shouldn't answer—clinical questions, conflicting sources, corpus gaps.

I documented the full 0→1 product package—not just a slide. There's an opportunity assessment, PRD with explicit non-goals, agent architecture, a 50-scenario golden dataset, a 30-document sample knowledge base, evaluation scorecard with sample runs, metrics framework, rollout plan, and Figma-ready UI specs.

Design principles I kept central: humans decide, cite-or-silence, escalate-over-guess, and eval gates before corpus expansion.

This is intentionally anonymized. All metrics are sample or target values. I'm not claiming production deployment—I'm showing how I think about shipping assistive AI where accountability matters. If your team is building agentic or RAG products in regulated or high-stakes contexts, this is representative of how I'd lead the product work."
