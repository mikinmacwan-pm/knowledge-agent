# Retrieval Quality Training

## §2.1 Writing effective queries

**Good:** "Transitional housing programs ages 18–24 Region B"
**Poor:** "housing help"

Include: topic, geography, age/eligibility if known.

## §3.1 Verifying citations

Always open citation drawer for medium/low confidence responses before sharing with callers.
