# Confidence Score Guide

## How scores are calculated (simplified)

Confidence reflects retrieval match strength + source agreement + freshness — **not** answer correctness alone.

| Range | Label | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.75–1.0 | HIGH | Verify citations |
| 0.55–0.74 | MEDIUM | Verify before use |
| 0.00–0.54 | LOW | Escalate |
