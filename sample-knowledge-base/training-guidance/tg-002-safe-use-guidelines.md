# Safe Use Guidelines

## §1.2 Guarantee language

**Never promise** beds, benefits approval, or timelines based on agent output alone. Always verify with cited sources.

## §2.1 When to escalate

- Low confidence (<0.55)
- Conflicting sources
- Out-of-scope clinical or legal questions
- Caller safety concerns

## §2.2 PII

Do not paste client identifiers into the agent.
