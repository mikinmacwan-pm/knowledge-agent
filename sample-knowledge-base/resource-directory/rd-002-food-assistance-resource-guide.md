# Food Assistance Resource Guide

## §3.1 Emergency Food Box Program

Households facing immediate food insecurity may qualify for a one-time emergency food box.

**Income guidelines (2026):**
| Household size | Monthly gross limit |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | $1,950 |
| 2 | $2,640 |
| 3 | $2,800 |
| 4 | $3,380 |

Staff must verify income documentation. **Agent and staff cannot make final eligibility determinations.**

## §4.2 Mobile Food Distribution — Region B

| Day | Location | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tuesday | Northside Community Center | 10 AM – 12 PM |
| Thursday | Riverside Park lot | 2 PM – 4 PM |

Schedules update weekly. Check freshness date before citing to callers.

## §5.1 Program boundaries

This organization **does not deliver groceries** to homes. Refer callers to partner pantry programs or SNAP application support.
