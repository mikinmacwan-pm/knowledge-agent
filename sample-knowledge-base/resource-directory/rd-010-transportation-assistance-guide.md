# Transportation Assistance Guide

## §2.1 Medical appointment rides

Eligible clients may receive up to 4 one-way trips/month for medical appointments with 48-hour advance booking.

## §2.2 Bus pass program

Low-income households: monthly transit pass at reduced rate. Income verification required.

## §3.1 Paratransit referral

Clients with documented mobility limitations — refer to regional paratransit authority (555-0300).

## §4.1 Scope limits

Organization does not provide emergency ambulance transport. For medical emergencies, follow crisis protocol.
