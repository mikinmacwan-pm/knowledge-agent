# Veteran Services Resource Guide

## §2.1 Emergency Rental Assistance — VRAP

Veterans facing housing instability may qualify for Veterans Rental Assistance Program (VRAP).

- **Eligibility summary:** Honorable discharge, income at or below 80% AMI
- **Intake:** Complete VRAP checklist; benefits counselor verification required
- **No final eligibility determination by agent**

## §3.1 SSVF — Supportive Services for Veteran Families

SSVF provides rapid rehousing and prevention services. Intake steps in Housing SOP cross-reference.

## §4.1 Crisis routing

**Veterans Crisis Line (VCL):** **988, press 1** — use when veteran expresses crisis or suicidal ideation.

Use local crisis line (800-555-0199) when VCL unavailable or for non-veteran-specific crisis.
