# Domestic Violence Resource Guide

## §2.1 Confidential Safe Shelter Intake

Survivors seeking safe shelter may access **confidential intake** without abuser notification.

- Warm line: **800-555-0177** (24/7)
- Shelter locations disclosed only after safety screening (human-led)
- **Do not contact alleged abuser** under any circumstance

## §3.1 Safety planning

Safety plans must be authored by trained staff using the approved template. **Knowledge Agent must not generate safety plans.**

## §4.2 Return-home decisions

Staff must **not** advise callers to return home after violence disclosures. Escalate to supervisor for safety planning.

## §5.1 Legal referrals

Org scope: explain protective order process at high level only. Refer to legal aid partners — **no legal advice.**
