# Emergency Housing Resource Guide

## §2.4 Program Horizon — Young Adult Transitional Housing

Program Horizon serves adults **ages 18–24** with documented **Region B connection** (residency, school enrollment, or employer verification).

- **Type:** Transitional housing (30–90 day stays)
- **Intake:** Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- **Capacity:** Bed availability varies daily — **never guarantee placement**
- **Referral:** Complete intake form; supervisor sign-off for expedited cases

## §2.5 Crisis Shelter Pathway

For immediate shelter needs tonight, staff must contact the **Housing Line** at **800-555-0142** (24/7) to verify bed availability before sharing options with callers.

## §3.1 Region A — Warming Centers

During cold weather alerts (Nov–Mar), Region A warming centers operate 6 PM – 7 AM. See Seasonal Shelter Addendum for locations.
