# Behavioral Health Referral Directory

## §1.1 Crisis vs Non-Crisis Pathways

**Crisis (immediate safety concern):**
- Crisis Line: **800-555-0199** (24/7)
- Warm handoff required per Active Escalation SOP
- Staff must **not** assess risk level — escalate to supervisor

**Non-crisis outpatient:**
- Partnership Care pathway for anxiety, depression (outpatient)
- Region B clinics listed in §2.3
- No treatment recommendations — referral information only

## §4.1 Minor consent

Youth mental health services for minors require parental consent except where statute allows (see Youth Services Resource Guide).
