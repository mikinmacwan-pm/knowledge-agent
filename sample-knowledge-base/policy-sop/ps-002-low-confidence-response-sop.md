# Low Confidence Response SOP

## §2.1 Confidence thresholds

| Score | Staff action |
| --- | --- |
| ≥0.75 HIGH | Verify citations; may use |
| 0.55–0.74 MEDIUM | Verify all citations before sharing |
| <0.55 LOW | Escalate; do not rely on summary |

## §3.1 Medium confidence workflow

Staff must open at least one citation before client-facing use. Optional checkbox: "I verified before use."
