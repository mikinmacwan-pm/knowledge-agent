# Active Escalation SOP

## §4.2 Safety-adjacent behavioral health

When caller expresses hopelessness, self-harm ideation, or ambiguous safety concern:
1. Cite crisis line resources
2. **Do not assess risk level**
3. Escalate to supervisor immediately
4. Document in case system per Documentation SOP

## §4.3 Domestic violence disclosures

Escalate all active DV safety questions. Agent may cite shelter resources; human leads safety planning.

## §6.1 Crisis housing verification

Before promising any bed: contact Housing Line (800-555-0142). Never guarantee placement.
