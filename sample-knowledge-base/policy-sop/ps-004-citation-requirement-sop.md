# Citation Requirement SOP

## §2.1 Cite-or-silence rule

Every factual sentence in agent output must map to an approved source citation. Uncited claims are prohibited.

## §3.1 Citation format

Inline numeric citations [1][2] linking to document_id, section, and last_reviewed date.

## §4.1 Staff verification

Staff responsible for verifying citation accuracy before client-facing use.
